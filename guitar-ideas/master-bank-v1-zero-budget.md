# 💸 Master Bank v1 — Zero-Budget Cut

**Your rule:** I have €0. Kill anything that requires paying to build or launch.

**How the cut works (3 rules):**
1. ✅ **Build with free tools only** — open source, free tiers, no paid APIs, no paid plugins, no hardware purchases.
2. ✅ **Launch with €0** — free hosting (GitHub Pages / Cloudflare / Vercel free tier), free subdomain, no domain purchase needed, web/PWA/desktop first (no $99/yr Apple account until money exists).
3. ✅ **Personal use — your content, your call.** Approved = anything that permits personal use: open source (MIT/Apache/ISC/MPL), public domain, CC (including NC), and freeware whose EULA says "free for personal use". **Music: no content restriction for a personal build** — point your tools at any music you have; that is ordinary personal use. Commercial licences are never required. The one line that remains: if a project ever goes to other people (published, sold, hosted for them), what's inside it must be the builder's own, public domain, CC, crowd-sourced or user-supplied. Still never approved, for practical reasons: cracked software (malware), time-limited trials as a base, and unqualified student licences.

**Parked, not deleted:** anything killed below can be revived later once there's money. The kill ledger tells you exactly what to avoid for now and why.
